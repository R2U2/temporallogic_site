# Wayback recovery

Recovered locally on 2026-09-11 from the Internet Archive Wayback Machine.

Original page: http://ti.arc.nasa.gov/m/profile/kyrozier/benchmarking_scripts/benchmarking_scripts.html

Main-page snapshot: https://web.archive.org/web/20090118085518/http://ti.arc.nasa.gov/m/profile/kyrozier/benchmarking_scripts/benchmarking_scripts.html

The page is “Model Checking Benchmarking Scripts” by Kristin Y. Rozier (2007),
supporting the LTL Satisfiability Checking experiments with M. Y. Vardi.

## Recovered material

- All 81 unique successfully archived URLs returned by the Wayback CDX query
  for the original benchmarking_scripts directory: pages, stylesheets,
  equation images, ZIP downloads, LaTeX source, and software agreement.
- Each file uses its earliest successful capture returned by that query;
  these are mixed capture dates, not one simultaneous site snapshot.
- The 460 individual formula files were extracted from the recovered
  formulas.zip so the formula listing works locally. The ZIP is also retained.
- recovery-manifest.json records original URLs, snapshot timestamps, archive
  URLs, CDX digests, downloaded SHA-256 hashes, and final local SHA-256 hashes.

## Local adjustments

- Added index.html as a redirect to benchmarking_scripts.html.
- Changed absolute links to the old NASA formula directory into relative links.
- Changed the NASA software-agreement link to the recovered local agreement.
- Reused two identical assets for node6_files (the stylesheet and img3.png),
  matching their Wayback CDX digests to recovered copies in other chapters.
- Original document content and historical external references are retained.
  External sites may no longer be available.

The software's recovered license terms are in software_agreement.txt.
No recovered scripts were executed. Nothing was published as part of recovery.
